# Discovery and Gate A

Status: prepared; no participant sessions conducted.

## Recruitment and consent

Recruit 10–15 Persian-speaking older adults with varied smartphone experience, eyesight, motor ability, and literacy. Record preferred language and device/navigation style, not names in the research sheet. Invite family helpers for the setup study only with the older adult's agreement. Explain the study in Persian, obtain consent, allow stopping at any point, and obtain separate consent for any recording. Use participant IDs. Do not collect real phone numbers, private messages, or contact photos in research reports.

Suggested opening: «می‌خواهیم ببینیم کار با این صفحه چقدر آسان است. شما را امتحان نمی‌کنیم؛ برنامه را بررسی می‌کنیم. هر وقت بخواهید می‌توانید متوقف شوید.»

## Session, approximately 30–45 minutes

1. Explain consent, independence, and stopping. Record device model, Android version, navigation mode, font scale, and prior smartphone experience.
2. Baseline on their existing launcher: find a pre-agreed person, reach the dialer without placing a call, open a requested non-private app, and return Home.
3. Introduce the prototype without teaching the task sequence. Use test contact details controlled by the facilitator; never place a real call during timed tasks.
4. Repeat equivalent tasks. Counterbalance order across participants where feasible to reduce learning effects. Record prior exposure and any accessibility accommodations.
5. Observe a family helper setting up contacts and text size with the older adult's input. Time from first in-app screen to a usable home; exclude download/install and third-party authentication.
6. Ask the participant to explain which screen they are on, how they would go Home, and whom the help button would call. Ask what wording or button caused confusion.

Prompts: «شمارهٔ این فرد را برای تماس آماده کنید.» / «این برنامه را باز کنید.» / «به صفحهٔ اصلی برگردید.» Do not name buttons or point to controls during the independent attempt. Intervene for discomfort or safety, record the intervention, and do not count the attempt as independent success.

## Measurement definitions

- Call task starts when the task prompt ends on Home; ends when the correct number is visible in the system dialer. No call connection is measured.
- App task ends when the correct app is visible; returning Home is a separate task.
- Record completion, elapsed seconds, tap count, mis-taps, facilitator help, abandonments, and exact confusing wording. Agree a timeout before sessions and record timed-out attempts as failures, not missing data.
- Report independent completion rate, all denominators, median successful-call time, and failure/timeout counts. Show baseline and prototype separately; do not discard unsuccessful attempts to claim overall improvement.
- Suggested record fields: participant ID, exposure, device/OS/navigation/font, interface, task, success, time, taps, mis-taps, assistance, observation, proposed fix.

## Gate A decision

Review whether the prototype improves independent task completion over baseline and whether critical confusion recurs. Classify findings by severity: unsafe/wrong person or number; cannot complete/return Home; recoverable delay; cosmetic. Revise recurring critical failures before expanding the application. Document evidence, changes, and a proceed/revise decision from the product owner.

Gate A has no fabricated numeric pass threshold. Later RFP acceptance requires at least 15 participants and its published targets; it is separate from this discovery study.

## Device matrix to arrange

Reserve at least six real phones from at least three manufacturers, including common Samsung and Xiaomi models plus a third locally relevant brand. Include an Android 9 device and a current Android device, budget and mid-range hardware, button and gesture navigation. Record actual model/API/RAM instead of pre-marking coverage. Emulator results do not count as physical-device acceptance.
